Vision, about, current research projects

探索 MAY Lab 的願景與核心使命。

The vision of the MAY Lab is to advance rigorous, impactful research aimed at improving mental health outcomes among adolescents, young adults, and their families, guided by a diversity, equity, inclusion, and accessibility framework.

Our work centers on two components aimed at reducing the duration of untreated illness in youths and young adults. First, we employ a developmental, transdiagnostic framework to improve early identification of psychopathology to improve long-term outcomes. Second, we adopt a culturally informed approach to identify and address socio-cultural barriers that contribute to mental health stigma and hinder help-seeking.

Research Projects

1. Modeling the Early Dynamic Development of Psychopathology: The Use of Longitudinal Network Analyses from a Transdiagnostic Framework

2. Developing a Theoretical Framework of Mental Illness Stigma in East Asia: A Mixed-Methods Study.
